# Lab 1: the site

## Content checklist (chapter 2.4)

1. The home section has exactly two sentences.
2. Each project has What, Why and Result, and a link or a line starting "No link because".
3. The about story is one specific moment, not a list of traits.
4. The ask names one action and one way to reach you.
5. `grep -inE "todo|lorem|project 1|project 2|passionate|welcome to" content/site.md` prints nothing.
6. You can point at the line that proves each of the five above.

## Article outline (chapter 3.10)

- Title: a problem you had
- Problem: what you expected, what happened
- What I tried: two or three attempts, including failures, with the command or output
- What worked and why
- What I would do differently
