# About

The first AI Chef I built for SaveBite was just a chat box: it could suggest recipes, but it had no idea what was in your fridge. I rewrote it to load your real fridge items, put the ones about to expire at the top of the prompt as "use these first", and ask your cuisine, diet and cooking time before it suggests anything. I also kept the fridge list in the conversation, so a follow-up like "can I skip the eggs?" still knows what you have.
