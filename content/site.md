# About

At first, SaveBite was going to have a separate recipe section and a separate expiry list, so users would check what's expiring and then search for a recipe themselves. We realised that's two jobs most people won't bother doing, so we automated it: the AI Chef reads what's in your fridge, picks the food expiring soonest, and suggests a recipe that uses it after asking your cuisine, diet and cooking time.

# SaveBite

My team of three built a Flutter app that tracks the food in your fridge, reminds you before it expires, and suggests recipes from what you already have.

When families buy groceries in bulk and life gets busy, we forget what we already bought, and we only find it when it has already gone bad.

A friend tried it on her own fridge, and it reminded her about food that was about to expire, so she used it first instead of throwing it away.

[Figma prototype](https://www.figma.com/proto/E1D23xc6P5X4TbzC1G8ie2/SaveBite?node-id=0-1). The code is in a private team repo.

# Attendance Management System

For my EAD semester project, I built an ASP.NET Core MVC app with SQL Server where admins set up courses and timetables, teachers mark attendance for their own classes, and students see their timetable and attendance record.

Attendance was taken on paper, so students could not check their own record and teachers worked out percentages by hand.

My instructor tested every feature, then removed the login and security tokens from requests in the browser's Network tab, and the app rejected each one instead of showing the page or saving the form.
