This is the demo proposal. It is shown to everyone and records nothing.

## Ask

I would like twenty minutes to show you one small change that could make your weekly report easier to trust.

## Reason

You wrote that your team spends Monday mornings fixing the numbers in the weekly report. That is the problem I enjoy most.

## Fit

I built a small tool for exactly this kind of problem. You can read how it works in [the example project](/go/example-project).

## Trade-offs

It will not fix the source of the bad numbers. It makes them visible, which is the first step, and it needs one hour a week from someone on your team.
